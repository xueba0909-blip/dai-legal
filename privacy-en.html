Please export backups. **(b) Optional cloud sync / account linking** If you enable sync or link an account, relevant content or identity metadata may be transmitted and stored with us or our infrastructure providers (for example, for sign-in). If you choose “Not now,” you can still journal on this device. You can turn sharing off anytime in **Settings → Share data with third-party AI**. --- ### 6.
