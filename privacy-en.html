Privacy Policy for Dai - AI Voice Diary

Effective date: September 8, 2026
Controller / Operator: Qiror (Xiaolei Zhang) (“we,” “us,” or “Dai”)

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect information when you use the Dai mobile application (listed on the App Store as “Dai - AI Voice Diary”) (the “App”). By using the App, you acknowledge that you have read this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

This Policy alone is not how we obtain permission to send your content to third-party AI. The App shows an in-app disclosure and asks for your permission before any personal content is sent to a third-party AI service. You can decline and still record, type, and save entries on this device.

1. Scope

This Policy applies to the App on iOS and Android, including voice journaling, optional AI transcription and editing (after in-app permission), mood analysis, tags, weekly summaries, on-device storage, automatic anonymous account creation, optional Apple or Google account linking, in-app subscriptions, and crash reporting. The App does not offer journal cloud sync. The current version does not send diary reminder notifications.

2. Information We Collect

(a) Content you create (stored on this device): voice recordings; transcripts and AI-edited diary text; mood scores/tags, keywords, insights, weekly reports; nickname and avatar; photos you attach to a capture; content you export or share (for example share cards).

(b) Permissions and related data

  • Microphone: to record voice notes on this device (only with your permission).
  • Camera: to attach a photo to a capture when you choose to.
  • Photo Library: to attach photos to captures and to save share cards when you choose to.

The App does not request notification permission for diary reminders in the current version.

(c) Account identifiers

When cloud authentication is configured, the App automatically creates an anonymous session on launch (Supabase Auth). That session is an account identifier. It is not “no account until you enable cloud sync.” You may later link Sign in with Apple or Google to the same session so you can restore that cloud identity on another device. Linking does not upload your journal library for sync.

(d) Purchases

If you subscribe, Apple or Google processes payment. We use RevenueCat to validate receipts and entitlements. Those parties receive purchase and app-user identifiers, not diary text.

(e) Limited device / technical data

  • Device type, OS version, app version, language, locale, time zone
  • Crash and diagnostic data via Sentry when that tooling is enabled in the build (stack traces, device/app version). Default PII is off. Diary text is not sent.

(f) What we do not seek

  • We do not sell your personal information
  • We do not require government ID
  • We do not collect contacts or precise location for advertising in the current version

3. How We Collect It

Journal content is collected when you record, type, attach a photo, edit a profile, export, or tap an AI action after you have given permission. We do not silently upload diary text in the background. The anonymous auth session is created automatically at launch. AI payloads leave the device only after you tap Allow.

4. How We Use Information

  • Record, store, display, search, and manage your captures and diaries on this device
  • Transcribe speech and organize notes only after you give in-app permission to share data with third-party AI
  • Generate optional insights or weekly summaries you request, after that same permission
  • Authenticate the AI proxy and optional account linking (anonymous session; Apple/Google if you choose)
  • Validate Pro subscriptions
  • Improve reliability (crash reports) where Sentry is enabled
  • Respond to support requests and comply with law

We do not use your diary content to serve personalized ads, and we do not sell your diary content to advertisers. There is no setting that uploads your journals for cloud backup or sync.

5. Storage

(a) On-device journals

Journals, audio, attached photos, mood/weekly data, and profile data are stored locally. Uninstalling the App or clearing app data may permanently delete local content. Please export backups.

(b) Cloud account (automatic anonymous session)

Supabase stores the anonymous (or later linked) Auth user. We do not store your diary library there. The session JWT may be sent to our Cloudflare Worker when the App talks to the AI proxy (or a shared proxy token may be used instead, depending on configuration).

(c) Third-party AI processing (only with permission)

Audio and/or text leave this device for AI processing only after you tap Allow on the in-app permission screen (during onboarding, before the first send, or later in Settings). If you choose “Not now,” you can still journal on this device. You can turn sharing off anytime in Settings → Share data with third-party AI.

If you allow sharing, we send only what that request needs:

9. Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only as needed for the processors named in this Policy, for legal compliance, protection of rights and safety, or a business transfer (successor bound by this Policy).

10. Retention

  • On-device journals/audio/photos/profile: Under your control; deleted when you delete entries or wipe data in-app.
  • Auth account: While the anonymous or linked user exists. After you use in-app Delete account, the Auth user is removed from active systems typically within 30 days (backups up to 90 days). A new anonymous session may be created if you keep using the App.
  • AI request payloads we transmit: Sent for that request; we do not keep a separate training corpus. Provider retention follows their policy.
  • Support emails: Typically up to 24 months.
  • Diagnostics/logs (if any): Typically up to 12 months.

11. Your Rights and Choices

Subject to applicable law (e.g., GDPR, CCPA, PIPL), you may:

  • Access and view your data in-app
  • Correct entries and profile
  • Export a copy (e.g., JSON)
  • Delete entries, wipe local data, or use Settings → Delete account (deletes the cloud Auth user and local diaries/audio). This does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription; manage that in system subscription settings.
  • Decline AI sharing and still use on-device journaling
  • Withdraw AI sharing later in Settings → Share data with third-party AI
  • Disable microphone, camera, or photos in system settings
  • Contact xueba0909@gmail.com or your local regulator

We aim to respond to verifiable requests within 30 days.
